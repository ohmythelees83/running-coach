# Knee update — 26 Oct 2026

Follow-up to `2026-10-11-knee-note.md`. Significant episode since the last
check-in (13 Oct) — recording the whole arc here, not just today's state.

## What happened, in order
- **11 Oct:** last run before the gap (back-to-back day 2, clean).
- **~15–21 Oct:** roughly a week mostly off training. Alex's own words:
  "things weren't feeling right." This followed directly from the 11 Oct
  "not feeling 100%, hard to explain" report — in hindsight, that vague
  signal was an early read on something real, not nothing.
- **19 Oct:** cortisone injection in the knee, as scheduled.
- **22–25 Oct:** gradual return — two easy Zwift spins, then two runs
  (23 Oct, 25 Oct), both at modest distance.

## State as of 26 Oct
Reported directly: knee felt fine after the 25 Oct run, and is now "the
best it's ever been" since the issue started roughly a year ago. Swelling
has reduced to practically nothing. This is a genuine, positive shift —
not just "manageable" or "not 100%," a real change in direction.

## What this does and doesn't change
- **Does** support being less conservative about the physical
  progression gate than two weeks ago — the knee itself is in better
  shape than it's been through this whole block.
- **Does not** mean jumping straight back to pre-gap volume or structure.
  25 Oct's run (the second one back) used a different, more ambitious
  structure than the proven 1.25mi:250m ratio — longer segments, much
  shorter walk breaks — and showed the same HR-drift pattern flagged
  before (second segment sustained in Z3, poor recovery in the short walk
  break). That's a fitness/pacing caution, separate from the knee itself:
  12 days without a run is enough to lose some of the conditioning that
  made 123–135 sustainable across 3 reps, regardless of how good the
  joint feels.
- ACWR is reading elevated (1.32) purely because the 4-week baseline is
  depressed from the gap — a known trap, not a sign of doing too much in
  absolute terms. Still worth building back in rather than resuming at
  the pre-gap level immediately.

## Decision for 26 Oct–1 Nov
Treat this week as a genuine rebuild of consistency at the
already-proven ratio (1.25mi:250m, matching 23 Oct's clean session), not
a resumption of where things left off on 11 Oct. No back-to-back, no
progression, modest volume. See `training/2026-10-26-week.md`.

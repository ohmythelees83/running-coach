# HR drift trend — 15 Nov 2026

Flagging a pattern across this week's continuous runs, not any single
session.

## What actually happened (vs. the 9–15 Nov plan)
- **Mon 9 Nov** (plan said rest): ran anyway, ~4.1mi continuous. Genuinely
  clean — every mile split stayed in Z2, never exceeded 136bpm. The best
  effort-control result of the block so far.
- **Tue 10 Nov** (plan said continuous run): skipped, no activity.
- **Thu 12 Nov**: ~4.1mi continuous. Miles 1–3 fine, but the final mile
  drifted into Z3 (137.6 avg, 142 max).
- **Sat 14 Nov** (plan said ~4.5mi): came in shorter at 3.66mi, but drift
  started earlier and went deeper — Z3 by mile 2, sustained through mile 3
  and the finish (up to 143.9 avg, 147 max). Fastest pace of the week
  (9:24/mi), which likely explains both the shorter distance and the
  earlier drift.
- **Sun 15 Nov** (plan said rest): the big one — ~6.1mi, ~60 minutes
  continuous, 148m of elevation gain (the hilliest route run this block).
  Started conservatively (Z1 for the first 2 miles) but climbed steadily
  into sustained Z3 for the back half — miles 4, 5, and 6 all averaged
  in Z3, mile 6 hitting 145 avg/152 max, closing in on the lab-tested max
  of 165.

## The pattern
Each session this week ran longer and/or faster than planned, and the
point where HR drifts into Z3 got earlier and more severe as the week
went on — not a one-off. Today's run, specifically, combined three things
at once: the longest continuous distance yet, real hills for the first
time, and no rest day beforehand (ran Saturday too). That's three new
variables stacked in one session, not one.

No pain or swelling reported as of this check-in, but this wasn't asked
about specifically for today's run given how much harder it looks than
the rest of the week — worth a direct follow-up.

## What this changes
Next week holds distance where it's already proven (not the ~5mi step up
previously drafted), prioritizes a genuinely easy pace over distance,
restores actual rest days (no more surprise runs on Mon/Sun), and keeps
routes flat until effort control is solid again. See
`training/2026-11-16-week.md`.

# Continuous running milestone — 8 Nov 2026

Follow-up to `2026-10-26-cortisone-update.md`. The rebuild since the
cortisone injection has gone further than planned, in a good way.

## What happened since 26 Oct
- **26 Oct–1 Nov (the planned rebuild week):** ran close to the actual
  plan — 27 Oct and 29 Oct both held the proven 1.25mi:250m ratio
  cleanly (HR averaged 130.6/132.4 across reps, no Z3 drift). 31 Oct's
  session stayed at 2 reps rather than progressing to 3 as drafted —
  sensible caution, not a problem.
- **2–8 Nov:** sessions started extending run segments and shrinking
  walk breaks on their own — 3 Nov and 5 Nov both used ~1.75mi segments
  with only ~30-second breaks, well past the old 1.25mi:250m structure.
  5 Nov held HR in Z2 throughout despite the much shorter recovery.
- **7 Nov: first fully continuous run since the knee issue started** —
  no walk breaks at all, ~3.66mi in ~35 minutes. HR held in Z2 for the
  first ~3 miles (127.9 → 132.0 → 133.4bpm across the three full-mile
  splits), drifting into Z3 only in the final partial mile (138.9 avg,
  145 max) — about what you'd expect from fatigue at the end of a first
  continuous effort, not a red flag.
- **Reported today:** everything feels fine, no swelling or pain.

## One data-quality flag, not a training flag
3 Nov's session was logged with no chest strap connected — same known
issue as 26 Sep. Its average HR read 147, max 175 (above even the lab-
tested max of 165), while pace and power were entirely normal for the
effort. That's the optical wrist sensor misreading, not a real
physiological spike. Treat that session's HR as unreliable; its
relative_effort score (40, vs ~9–10 for comparable sessions) is
consequently also overstated.

## What this changes going forward
The run/walk ratio method did its job — it's how the base got rebuilt
from zero after the layoff and surgery, and it's genuinely enabled this.
But it's been superseded for easy-pace running: Alex is running
continuously now, by choice, and it's working. Training structure moves
to continuous-run duration progression from here, with run/walk kept in
back pocket only as a fallback if a session doesn't feel right that day
— not a resumed default. Reflected in `athlete-profile.md`'s Signature
Approaches section and this week's plan.

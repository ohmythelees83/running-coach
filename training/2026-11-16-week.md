## What actually happened, 9–15 Nov

rest) was genuinely clean — ~4.1mi, every mile held in Z2, no drift at
all. Tuesday's planned session was skipped entirely. Thursday's ~4.1mi
drifted into Z3 only in the final mile. Saturday came in shorter
(3.66mi) but faster, and drifted earlier and deeper — Z3 by mile 2.
Then Sunday (today, also meant to be rest): ~6.1mi, ~60 minutes
continuous, 148m of climbing — the longest, hilliest, and least
controlled session yet, sustained in Z3 for the back three miles,
peaking at 145bpm average / 152 max.

**The read:** enthusiasm is outrunning pacing. Each session this week
ran longer or faster than planned, and the point where HR drifts into Z3
got earlier each time — not a coincidence, a trend. Today specifically
stacked three new variables at once (longest distance yet, real hills
for the first time, no rest day before it) rather than testing one at a
time, which is exactly the hard/easy alternation principle this plan
leans on everywhere else. Total week volume came in at ~18mi against a
~12.1mi plan — a genuine overshoot, not the conservative rebuild that
was intended.

**No pain or swelling reported** as of the last check-in (8 Nov), but
today's run specifically hasn't been asked about yet given how much
harder it looks than the rest of the week on paper.

- **Distance holds, doesn't extend** — ~4–4.5mi, where Monday's clean
  session already proved comfortable. The previous draft's ~5mi step up
  is deliberately shelved until effort control is solid again.
- **Pace matters more than distance this week.** "Easy" should mean
  genuinely conversational — if Saturday's faster pace is what pulled the
  drift earlier, the fix is slower, not shorter.
- **Flat routes only** — save hills for once pacing on flat ground is
  reliably clean.
- **Rest days are rest days.** No more spontaneous runs on days the plan
  marks rest — if the legs feel good on a Monday or Sunday, that's worth
  mentioning before adding a session, not after.
- **Still no back-to-back** — unchanged reasoning from previous weeks.

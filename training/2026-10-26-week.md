## Where things actually stand

the issue started roughly a year ago — swelling down to practically
nothing, no pain after either return run. The cortisone injection (19 Oct)

**What actually happened since 13 Oct:** 14 Oct's planned London run
became an easy Zwift spin instead, then ~15–21 Oct was mostly off
training ("things weren't feeling right" — in hindsight, a continuation
of 11 Oct's vague signal, not a false alarm). The injection was 19 Oct.
Return began 22 Oct (easy spin), then two runs: 23 Oct (3.44mi, clean —
held the proven 1.25mi:250m ratio, HR controlled throughout) and 25 Oct
(3.95mi, built on a different, more ambitious structure — longer ~15min
segments with only ~60s walk breaks — which showed the same HR-drift
pattern from a few weeks back: the second segment sustained in Z3
(137.9bpm avg), and the short walk break didn't bring it back down).

**The honest read:** the knee itself is genuinely better. But 12 days
without a run is enough to lose some of the conditioning that made
123–135 sustainable across 3 reps on the old ratio — that's a fitness
question, separate from the joint. ACWR is also reading elevated (1.32)
right now, purely because the 4-week baseline is depressed from the gap —
a known trap after time off, not a sign of overdoing it in absolute
terms. Both point the same way: rebuild consistency deliberately this
week, don't resume at the 11 Oct level just because the knee allows it.

**Availability this week:** off on leave, can train any day — genuinely
flexible for the first time in a few weeks.

## This week's shape: rebuild, don't resume

- **Back to the proven ratio only** — 1.25mi run : 250m walk, the
  structure that was clean on 23 Oct. Not Sunday's longer-segment,
  shorter-walk version.
- **No back-to-back this week** — durability isn't in question, but
  stacking two running days right after a 12-day gap isn't the right
  first test. One at a time.
- **No progression** — same ratio as before the gap, not an attempt to
  pick up where the walk-shrink experiment (200m) left off. That resumes
  once a couple of weeks are banked clean again.
- **Modest volume** — closer to a fresh return-to-running ramp than a
  continuation of late-September/early-October's ~15–17mi weeks.

## Where things actually stand

**The real headline: continuous running has arrived.** Across the two
weeks since the cortisone injection (19 Oct), the proven 1.25mi:250m
run/walk structure held clean on 27 Oct and 29 Oct, then sessions started
stretching the run segments and shrinking the walk breaks on their own —
~1.75mi segments with only ~30-second breaks by 3 and 5 Nov. That
culminated in **7 Nov: the first fully continuous run since the knee
issue started**, ~3.66mi with no walk breaks at all, HR controlled in Z2
for the first ~3 miles before drifting into Z3 only in the final partial
mile — normal fatigue, not a flare. No pain or swelling reported since,
including today.

**One data-quality note:** 3 Nov's session logged with no chest strap
connected (same issue as 26 Sep) — its HR (avg 147, max 175) is
unreliable and its relative_effort score overstated as a result. Pace
and power that day were entirely normal.

**What this changes:** the run/walk method is retired as the default for
easy running (see the updated Signature Approaches note in
`athlete-profile.md`) — not because it failed, but because it worked.
Sessions from here build continuous-run duration directly.

**Where the numbers actually sit:** 3-week average volume is ~10.1mi/wk
— still well down from the ~15mi/wk before the gap, which is expected
and fine; it's being rebuilt, not resumed. ACWR reads 1.56 (the "high"
band) using Strava's raw numbers, but that's almost entirely the 3 Nov
HR-strap issue inflating that one session — correcting for it, ACWR is
closer to ~1.0, solidly normal. Worth knowing the real number, not the
artifact.

## This week's shape: build continuous duration, modestly

- **Continuous running is the default now**, not a progression target to
  earn back — Alex has already proven it works. That said: no shame in a
  walk break on a day that doesn't feel right. It's a fallback, not a
  failure.
- **Pacing note on the Z3 tail-end drift** (both 25 Oct and 7 Nov showed
  it): start each run a notch easier than feels necessary — aim Z1 for
  the first mile specifically — to leave more room before fatigue-drift
  sets in late, rather than running near the Z2 ceiling from the start.
- **Still no back-to-back** — one clean weekend is banked (10/11 Oct),
  but that was a month ago now and a real gap sits in between. Hold off
  one more week on stacking two days, let continuous running itself get
  a track record first.
- **Volume stays conservative** — barely above last week's actual
  (~11.8mi), not a resumption of pre-gap levels. The shift to continuous
  running is itself the meaningful change this week; it doesn't need a
  volume jump stacked on top of it.

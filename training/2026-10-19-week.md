General pattern for a cortisone injection (confirm against what you're
actually told): the joint is often rested for the first 24–48 hours,
avoiding strenuous or high-impact load while any procedure-related
irritation ("cortisone flare" — temporary increased soreness, not a sign
of failure) settles. The anti-inflammatory effect itself can take several
days, sometimes up to a week or two, to fully develop. Many clinicians
advise holding off running specifically for about a week, though this
varies by joint, dose, and the reason for the injection — hence asking
the doctor directly rather than assuming.

## What this draft assumes, and why it's a cutback either way

23–29 Nov will be the **5th straight week of volume growth** (26 Oct →
2 Nov → 9 Nov → 16 Nov → 23 Nov), with no recovery week yet — that was
flagged directly before 23–29 Nov was built, and Alex chose to proceed
anyway. Whatever 23–29 Nov's pace-test result is, this draft recommends
**30 Nov–6 Dec be the overdue recovery week** (~20–30% volume cut),
because the periodization reasoning behind that doesn't depend on the
result — 5 build weeks in a row either way:

- **If the slower pace held Z1–Z2 clean all week:** genuinely good news —
  it confirms pace was the lever, not terrain. That's exactly the result
  worth consolidating with an easier week before building further on top
  of it, rather than immediately stacking a 6th build week on a result
  that's one week old.
- **If sessions still drifted even at a deliberately slow pace:** that
  rules out pace as the sole explanation and points at something else —
  fueling, sleep, heat, or cumulative fatigue from 5 straight weeks of
  growth. A lighter week is also the right move here, both to let fatigue
  genuinely clear before troubleshooting further, and because pushing
  volume into an unresolved, worsening pattern is the overtraining risk
  this file exists to flag.

Either way, **no back-to-back reintroduced yet** — that waits for a clean
single long run first, consistent with one variable at a time.

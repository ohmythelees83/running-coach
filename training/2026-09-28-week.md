**What this changes:** nothing about the physical progression rule (no

## HR targets for this block (unchanged)
- **Easy / walk segments:** ≤122 bpm (Z1)
- **Run segments:** 123–135 bpm (Z2) — this is the main aerobic training
  zone for this phase
- **Avoid sustained Z3+ (136 bpm+)**

## New non-negotiable: route choice on controlled-effort days
On any session that isn't a deliberate hard effort (which is all of them
right now — zero quality sessions), avoid routes with live segments you
know well, particularly the parkrun course. If a HR alert/cap is available
on your watch, use it. The goal is boring, controlled effort — the same
discipline the race itself will demand on a course that "feels" fast.

## Progression rule (unchanged)
Continue extending the run segment / shortening the walk segment only if
the prior session produced no knee swelling or pain. Resumes this week —
Saturday's effort-control issue doesn't override this, since the trigger is
specifically pain/swelling.

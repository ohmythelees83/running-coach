## What changed from the original draft, and why

**5–11 Oct went well.** Tuesday 6 Oct's walk-shrink step (250m→200m) held
clean — all 3 reps stayed in Z2, no repeat of the drift pattern. Saturday
10 Oct's back-to-back day 1 was the real breakthrough: HR held in Z2
across all three reps (128.3→134.1→134.8 avg) for the first time this
block, a clean pass on "holding 123–135 for real" after 26 Sep's miss.

**But this week has three real constraints, not one:**
1. **London Wed/Thu for work** — turbo isn't available there, so the
   week's structured run moves to Wednesday morning in London, and
   Thursday becomes a forced rest day rather than the usual second run.
2. **Volunteering at a running event Saturday** — on your feet most of
   the day, not a training session itself, but real fatigue. The week's
   long run moves to Sunday instead.
3. **"Not feeling 100%, hard to explain"** — vague, not clearly described
   as swelling or pain, so this isn't the hard stop-rule's trigger. But it
   doesn't fit a clean pattern either, which matches what's already in
   your Coaching Philosophy about this knee issue (not clearly
   load-triggered, flares without an obvious cause). Logged properly for
   the first time in `health/2026-10-11-knee-note.md` — worth doing from
   here on rather than only mentioning it in passing. Given it, and given
   Monday 19 Oct's cortisone injection is one week out, this week **holds
   the walk back at 250m** rather than continuing at 200m — not because
   200m failed (it didn't), but because this isn't the week to be sitting
   at the newest, least-proven edge of anything.

Put together: no ratio progression, no back-to-back, and a genuinely
lighter week than even the original recovery draft — intentional, not
compensated for elsewhere.

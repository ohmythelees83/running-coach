## Why this is a recovery/consolidation week, not another build

Three weeks in a row have now each introduced a new stimulus: 21 Sep (the
first back-to-back), 28 Sep (ratio progression resumed to 1.25mi), and
5 Oct (second back-to-back plus the first walk-shrink step). That's the
standard "~3 build weeks, then a cutback" periodization pattern already
baked into this skill — and it lands at a good moment anyway, since it
gives the HR-drift pattern from 28 Sep–4 Oct a lighter week to settle in,
alongside Tuesday 6 Oct's direct read on it. Two reasons pointing the same
way is a good reason to actually take the week, not just a defensible one.

This is a deliberate, temporary step back from the Base-phase volume
target (~25mi+/week) — not away from it. Consolidating what's already been
gained (the longer run segment, the back-to-back track record, the first
walk-shrink step) is what makes the next build phase able to push further,
not a detour from it.

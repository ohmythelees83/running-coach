# Training Phase Roadmap — Thames Path 100 (May 2027)

A living reference, not a fixed calendar. Every date below is an estimate
built from typical phase lengths — what actually moves the plan from one
phase to the next is the exit criteria being met, checked against real
Strava data, not a date arriving. Update this file (and the Coaching
Philosophy pointer in `athlete-profile.md`) whenever a phase actually
changes, and say exactly what moved and why.

Race day: **Saturday 1 May 2027.** Today: 29 Sep 2026 — ~214 days out.

## Phase 1 — Base (current)

**Started:** ~23 Sep 2026 (Athlete OS / Coaching Philosophy formalized;
the graduated return to running itself began 31 Aug 2026).
**Target window:** now through ~mid-December 2026 (~11–12 weeks) — estimate only.

**What happens:** run/walk ratio progression, zero quality sessions,
aerobic base built in Zone 1/2, symptom-first knee monitoring, turbo for
non-impact cross-training, back-to-back long-run weekends introduced and
practiced. Grounded in Johnston's aerobic-base-first principle (Coaching
Philosophy Core Principle 1).

**Exit criteria — all of these, not just time elapsed:**
1. Weekly running volume sustained at ~25mi+/week for 3 consecutive weeks
   (currently ~14mi/week, 3-week avg 13.7mi)
2. At least 2 back-to-back weekends completed with **both** days genuinely
   effort-controlled — HR in the prescribed zone, not just distance
   matched (1 of 2 days achieved this so far, 26–27 Sep)
3. No knee swelling/pain for 4+ consecutive weeks
4. Continuous single-session running duration reaches 90+ minutes without
   notable HR drift/fade late in the session
5. ACWR has stayed in the 0.8–1.3 band for 3 consecutive weeks (not just
   touched it once)

## Phase 2 — Build

**Target window:** ~mid-December 2026 through early March 2027 (~10–11 weeks).

**What happens:** Johnston-style muscular-endurance block (weighted
hill/uphill work) introduced late-Base/early-Build; Britton's in-run
progressive-effort sessions; back-to-backs become routine rather than
novel; Evans-style speed work introduced once the aerobic/ME foundation
actually supports it — the first genuine quality sessions of the block;
weekly volume climbs toward its practical peak; long runs extend
significantly.

**Exit criteria:**
1. Longest single long run reaches ~3–4 hours continuous (or run/walk
   equivalent)
2. Race-day fueling strategy tested and refined across multiple long runs —
   a real carb-per-hour target established from data, not assumed from
   Evans' or Britton's numbers
3. At least 3–4 back-to-back weekends completed cleanly
4. Weekly volume has peaked near its practical ceiling for this build —
   informed by how the body actually responds, not a number fixed today

## Phase 3 — Race-Specific Prep (Peak)

**Target window:** ~early March through mid-April 2027 (~5–6 weeks).

**What happens:** course-specific long efforts mimicking Thames Path's
flat, repetitive, towpath demands (per the race dossier's finding that the
monotony itself is the difficulty); full race-day fueling rehearsal at the
Build phase's confirmed carb rate; night-running practice; gear/kit testing
against the race dossier's mandatory kit list; final volume peak.

**Exit criteria:** race-day fueling, gear, and pacing plan fully rehearsed
and confirmed; volume peak achieved without an injury flare.

## Phase 4 — Taper

**Target window:** ~mid-April through 1 May 2027 (~2.5–3 weeks).

**What happens:** standard ultra taper — volume drops significantly
(~40–60% off peak) while keeping some frequency/intensity to stay sharp;
focus shifts to logistics (travel, packet pickup, per the race dossier),
sleep, and final gear checks.

## How a phase transition actually gets decided

Not by the calendar — by a criteria review. Each time `/plan-my-week` or
`/update-dashboard` runs, check the current phase's exit criteria against
real Strava data. When they're genuinely met, say so explicitly, move the
"current phase" marker, and update this file plus the Coaching Philosophy
pointer in `athlete-profile.md` — stating exactly what changed. Don't
advance because a target date arrived if the criteria aren't met yet, and
don't hold back if they're met early.

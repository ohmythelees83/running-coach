## The decision this week makes

Alex pushed back on pace being the issue ("I feel ok running at that
pace") — fair, since Z3 at these zones (136–150bpm) is moderate, not hard,
so it wouldn't feel like effort. We agreed on a direct test rather than
debating feel vs. data further: **every easy session this week starts
deliberately slower than feels necessary — slower than the pace that's
been drifting — and the question is whether HR actually holds Z1–Z2 for
the whole session once pace genuinely drops.** If it does, pace was the
answer. If it still drifts at a genuinely slow pace, that's new
information (fatigue, heat, something else) and changes the read
entirely.

Alongside that, Alex asked for distance progression again this week —
~22mi total, with a long run of at least 8–9mi, and explicitly wants a
single long run this week rather than another back-to-back (three
attempts at a clean back-to-back weekend have produced one clean result;
stacking a fourth attempt on an unresolved pace question isn't where the
progression should go right now).

**Flagged directly, as the hard rules require:** this is the 5th straight
week of volume growth with no cutback (26 Oct 11.1 → 2 Nov 11.8 → 9 Nov
18.0 → 16 Nov 20.4 → this week's ~20–22mi), and Saturday's long run (8–9mi)
is the single biggest jump in one session this block has seen (previous
longest: 7.0mi, 21 Nov, which itself wasn't clean). Normally this is where
a recovery week gets proposed instead of another increase. Raised this
directly and Alex chose to proceed as asked, accepting the stacked
risk — noted here so it's on record, not buried.

# My Athlete OS

This is my personal coaching and training system, built around my real data
and goals. It lives in this project folder and connects to my Strava account
as the source of truth for my training data.

## Folders

- athlete-profile.md   — who I am: goals, races, constraints, and zones
- training/            — my training plans and weekly schedule
- reviews/             — weekly workout reviews and analysis (never overwritten)
- races/               — race research and race-day plans
- health/              — recovery, readiness, and injury-risk notes
- .claude/skills/      — reusable skills I can run with one command
- athlete-os.md        — this overview
